Privacy Policy

App: EPA 608 Prep (Android, package com.oldevenings.epa608prep)
Developer: oldevenings
Effective date: October 26, 2026

Summary

EPA 608 Prep works fully offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your study progress only on your own device: best scores, missed questions, bookmarks, a saved quiz in progress, your dark-mode choice and whether you have unlocked full access. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it anytime with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is a study aid for HVAC technicians and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.
